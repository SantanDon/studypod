# The weekly revision guide

## Monday
Pick three topics from your last lesson. Circle the topic you feel least ready to explain.

## Tuesday
Try answering from memory. Underline the passage in your notes that corrects or completes your answer.

## Wednesday
Revisit mistakes by covering the correction and answering again before checking your wording.

## Thursday
Write a new example for each topic and explain it aloud.

## Friday
Close your notes and take a three-question practice quiz. Mark each answer against its source and move one unfinished topic into next week.
